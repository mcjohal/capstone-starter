# Part 7. Add it to your Capstone page

The class site at **cruzjohal.com** gives every student a Capstone page to showcase their projects. You will add this project with its live link, its GitHub link, a screenshot, and a description. Allow about 15 minutes.

**You need:** your Render address (Part 6), your GitHub repository address, an authenticator app on your phone, and the **join code** your instructor gives the class.

## Take a screenshot

1. Open your live site on Render, on its Home or Browse page.
2. Windows: press **Windows + Shift + S**, drag a box around the page, then click the notice that appears and save the picture (the disk icon). Mac: press **Cmd + Shift + 4** and drag a box; the picture lands on your desktop.
3. Save it as a PNG or JPG under 5 MB.

## Sign in

1. Go to [www.cruzjohal.com/capstone](https://www.cruzjohal.com/capstone).
2. Click **Student sign in**.
3. Enter your email and click **Email me a sign in link**. There is no password.
4. Open the email and click the link. A page opens with a **Continue** button. Click it.
5. **The first time only:**
   1. Install an authenticator app on your phone if you don't have one: Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or Authy.
   2. Enter your name as it should appear on your page.
   3. In the app, tap the button to add an account, and scan the QR code on the screen.
   4. Type the 6 digit code the app shows and click **Verify & Finish Setup**.
6. **Every time after that:** type the current 6 digit code from the app and click **Verify**.

You land on **My Capstone**.

> The link in the email works once and expires after 15 minutes. If it says it has expired, go back to the sign in page and request a new one.

## Join your course

1. In the **My courses** box, type the join code your instructor gave the class. Capital letters or lowercase both work, with or without the dash.
2. Click **Join**. Your course appears in the list.

You only do this once per course.

## Add your project

1. Click **Add a project**.
2. Fill in the form:

   | Box | What to enter |
   |---|---|
   | Course | Your course |
   | Project title | Your site's name |
   | One line summary | One sentence for the project card |
   | Live project link | Your Render address, starting with `https://` |
   | Code link | Your GitHub repository address |
   | Description | A few short paragraphs: what the site does, your tables and how they connect, and one thing you learned. **Bold**, *italics*, and lists work, as in a GitHub README. |
   | Screenshot | The picture you saved |

3. Click **Save project**.

## Make it public

Your page starts **private**: only you and your instructor can see it. When it is ready to share:

1. On My Capstone, click **Make public**.
2. Click **View my page** to see what visitors see. With one project, it shows in full. With two or more, each shows as a card.

You can also click **My profile** to add a headline, a short About me, a photo, and links to your LinkedIn and GitHub. All of it is optional.

## Updating it later

Sign in again any time to edit the project. If you change your site, push to GitHub and Render updates the live link by itself; you only need to edit the Capstone page if the description or screenshot should change.
